# MathFormula v1.7 — Live and Closed

MathFormula now has only two top-level screens:
- **Live** — refreshed NSE opportunities discovered using the Groww connection
  already stored on your phone. Only actual qualified fresh research
  candidates are visible; no permanent TARIL, GAEL, ADVANCE shortcuts and
  no manual Refresh button.
- **Closed** — latest *captured* completed market-session review, previous
  selections and the Export Logs ZIP function. Every observed market price
  carries a time and is clearly **not** called an official closing price
  unless actually obtained from an official completed EOD source.

A complete universe refresh is attempted about every 15 minutes, and about
every 5 minutes after 14:30 IST, when the app is foregrounded. Selected
price quotes get more frequent light refreshes. The app automatically
chooses Live during regular market hours and Closed outside them; both
remain accessible.

**Alpha-30 v1.0 frozen 30-factor mathematical formula unchanged.** Price-only
technical screening cannot produce a verified BUY. An independently
deployed scanner is still needed for genuinely unattended NSE-wide coverage.
